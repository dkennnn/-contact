Who we are

A small studio with strong opinions about software.

Dkendev is a one-person indie studio based in Vietnam, shipping cross-platform apps and games since 2019. We pick projects that scratch a real itch — usually our own — and we hold them to a high bar: warm UX, real privacy, no dark patterns, no nagging.

We work with indie publishers, agencies and direct clients on mobile (Flutter), HTML5 games (Canvas / WebGL) and small native tools. Open to contract work, white-label builds, and partnership on the right idea.

01

Shipping beats perfection

Every project has a Friday-eve deadline. v1 is allowed to be ugly; it's not allowed to be late.

02

Offline-first by default

If your tool stops working when the wifi dies, we built it wrong. Local SQLite + optional sync is the pattern.

03

No dark patterns, no nags

Cancel buttons are real cancel buttons. Free tiers are honestly free forever. Paywalls don't hide behind fake "save" buttons.

04

One human, full ownership

From research to icon to App Store review reply. You always talk to the person who wrote the code.
